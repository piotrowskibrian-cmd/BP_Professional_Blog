Short bio

Brian Piotrowski is a Talent Acquisition leader with 15+ years building recruiting teams and modernizing the systems behind great hiring. He writes The Offer Letter and hosts the Candidate Spotlight podcast, sharing candid, real-world takes for recruiters, hiring leaders, and job seekers.

Full bio

Brian Piotrowski is a Talent Acquisition executive with more than 15 years of experience building high-performing recruiting teams and modernizing the systems, technology, and processes behind great hiring. He has led talent acquisition for large architecture, engineering, and construction firms, including a global TA organization hiring across North America, the Pacific, Saudi Arabia, and Latin America.

His career spans corporate teams and search firms, with hiring across AEC, Oil & Gas, Manufacturing, Environmental Services, IT, and Technology. He has also served on a corporate AI Governance Board, helping shape responsible automation and talent data strategy.

Through The Offer Letter, Brian challenges the "we've always done it that way" mindset in recruiting. On Candidate Spotlight, he gives job seekers a voice and shares the stories behind their resumes.
