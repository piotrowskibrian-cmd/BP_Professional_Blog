One common theme that has been heard through this pandemic, is how many people are helping their fellow neighbors in need. Individuals with lending libraries in their yards have been converted into mini food pantries. Individuals that are capable of running errands for less mobile or neighbor that are at-risk are getting groceries or supplies. We have all come together to help each in this time of crisis. As recruiters, how can we help?

First, let’s make sure we are doing what our governments are asking! Keep your distance! No one wants to add to this pandemic so let's keep social/physical distancing. These terms have been interchangeable but several government agencies and please stay inside. Yes, it sucks but it will pass soon.

If you are able to afford to have a meal at a restaurant, order out. Your money will go along way to help a local coffee shop or restaurant! It’s a small act but goes a long way for a small business to support rent and their staff.

Now the stuff recruiters are good at.

If someone is out of work, don't hesitate to review their resume. Most people are not professional resume writers and take that into account. Give a job seeker advice on their resume! Nowadays, resumes expand also social media profiles and to professional websites. Coach the candidate about their photo, bio, education, etc.

Teach this individual how to network to find their next job. Many of us have learned how to network (and have built our network) through years of practice. Start with small networking advice and keep building.

It’s probably been a while since the new job seeker has interviewed for a position. Be sure to help with interview prep, how to prepare for the interview, questions that maybe ask, and how to write a thank you message. It all small pieces of advice that help the larger picture of getting a new position.

As recruiters don't be afraid to help any job seeker out. Many people have lost their jobs last week and to the weeks to come. Try to do your part to help someone find their next job. Who knows, their company may be looking for a recruiter in the future and they will give you a call. Start networking :)
