Alright — let’s talk tariffs.

The sweeping tariffs announced by the Trump administration aren’t just about bumping up the price of your next Target run. They’re about to shake up the way companies operate, grow, and, yep — hire.

I’ll leave the deep business analysis to the economists, but let’s dig into what I do know: how this could play out in the U.S. hiring market.

White-Collar Jobs? Feeling the Squeeze.

When budgets get tight, cost centers — like marketing, HR, and support functions — are usually the first to feel the pinch. And with prices climbing on everything from computers to office supplies to fuel, companies are going to start looking very closely at headcount and other costs.

Even industries that feel miles away from manufacturing — think consulting, finance, tech — won’t be immune. If it costs more to run the basics, hiring freezes (and even some cuts) could be just around the corner. Alternatively, we will see possible salaries decrease to help drive more profit to the business.

Skilled Trades Are About to Have a Moment — But It’s Complicated.

Welders, electricians, toolmakers —  these trades have been in short supply for a while, and if reshoring manufacturing gains steam, demand is only going up.

BUT (and it’s a big but) — starting wages for trade apprenticeships are often very similar in salary. Plus, with materials costing more, manufacturers might choose to bring in temporary or contract workers instead of committing to full-time hires as salaries for temporary employees are cheaper. So, it’s a little “hurry up and wait” situation.

Expect U.S. Unemployment to Creep Up.

Higher costs = tougher decisions.

Some companies will hit pause on hiring. Others will freeze headcount altogether. And many will lean even harder on automation and AI to do the heavy lifting.

Bottom line? We could be staring down some layoffs and a bump in unemployment numbers in the not-so-distant future.

Growth Will Start to Look…More Global.

Companies are always on the hunt for talent that’s faster, smarter, and more affordable — and rising U.S. costs are only going to speed up that search overseas.

Nike recently made headlines shifting its growth focus beyond the U.S., and spoiler alert: they’re not going to be the only ones. Expect to see more big brands spreading their wings internationally.

Tariffs are about to throw some serious curveballs into the hiring market — some we’ll feel immediately, others that’ll roll out over time.

Companies will react differently, sure. But if you’re in Talent Acquisition, one thing’s crystal clear: flexibility and foresight are about to become your two new best friends.
