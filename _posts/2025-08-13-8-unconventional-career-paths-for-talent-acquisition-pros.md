The other week, the CEO of Perplexity, Aravind Srinivas, claimed that new advances in AI could soon make recruiters and executive assistants obsolete. While I respect technological progress, I’d argue that Mr. Srinivas doesn’t fully grasp what Talent Acquisition truly does or why it’s so valuable to organizations.

However, there’s no doubt AI is rapidly transforming talent acquisition, much like it is every profession. But let’s play along with the fear-mongering for a moment; what if AI really *did* make TA obsolete? What should we do next? Which skills transfer and where can recruiters and TA pros go from here?

The good news: the skills built in recruiting; relationship building, stakeholder management, problem solving, and strategic thinking, are prized across industries, are all skills that you need to master in TA. If you’re ready to look beyond traditional HR roles, here are eight unconventional career transitions:

**Product Management (especially at a SaaS company)**  
You've built skills for matching people with roles translates to building solutions for real user needs, managing stakeholder expectations, and prioritizing features. With an eye for business operations and the ability to collaborate with all people within an organization, you could be equipped to become a user-focused product manager driving innovation.

**Nonprofit or Social Enterprise Program Manager**

If you are done with for-profit companies you could shift to the nonprofit sector. Oversee impactful programs, manage teams, and foster partnerships for organizations that create social value. The experience gained with stakeholder engagement and project management translates directly.

**HR Tech Consultant**  
Put your insider knowledge of recruiting processes and HR tech to use by advising companies on selecting and rolling out the best tools. The ability to stay up-to-date on the best tools and help others avoid costly mistakes.

**Employer Branding Strategist**

Leverage the knowledge understanding the importance of employer value propositions and recruitment marketing to help organizations craft authentic, compelling employer brands. We live in a time where communication and storytelling have never been more important in attracting the best talent.

**Learning Experience Designer**

You have a deep understanding of skill gaps and organizational growth primes you to create engaging training content, onboarding modules, or digital learning journeys. If you’re interested in corporate training or EdTech, this is a smart launchpad.

**Corporate Social Responsibility Program Lead**

Today’s consumers (and employees) care about impact. With your internal knowledge of organizations and your ability to rally people behind a cause, you can lead CSR initiatives and build programs that make a difference.

Whether or not AI ever truly replaces TA, what we’ve just done here is skills-based career mapping. And there are countless ways you can adapt and thrive with your recruiting toolkit in entirely new arenas.
