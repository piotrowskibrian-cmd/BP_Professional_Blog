Did I just buy a web domain? What am I doing? My first blog post is coming out on my birthday? Now what?

One of my goals for 2020 is to get more involved in the talent acquisition community outside of my day job. Understanding that goal is not a S.M.A.R.T. goal, I am still keeping it as my goal. Never have I thought of myself as a good writer but, let's call this "self-development." I want to push myself outside my comfort zone and challenge the status quo. I am here to share my thoughts and opinions on the world of recruitment and talent acquisitions. Hopefully, this will let all of us grow as professions.

Recruiting has been a profession that has been around for centuries and I am not here to give you a history lesson on the profession. Much of what we as recruiters do - we've done the same for decades. Some of the tactics should die off and some other tactics we should keep (i.e. picking up the phone to call a candidate). New technologies keep being introduced into recruitment, some we should keep and others should end. We will explore the past, present, and future of recruiting including tech. Also, explore the ever-changing employment law surrounding recruiting.

The goal is to post one blog post weekly. It means 52 ideas, 52 topics, 52 posts! Feel free to share ideas, comments, and please send your feedback.

Here we go!
