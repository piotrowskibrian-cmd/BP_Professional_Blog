With layoffs, toxic cultures, and with the negative press about employers making heartless policies. We have already forgotten what the Great Resignation should have taught us as a society. In today's fast-paced and competitive business environment, employee retention is key. If you want to see crazy policies go to Redit's subreddit r/antiwork. You will be lost for hours reading terrible work policies, terrible coworkers, and terrible leaders. Employers need to start prioritizing their employees' well-being, including their mental health, paid time off (PTO), and work-life balance, tend to have happier, more productive, and loyal employees.

Mental health is a critical aspect of employee well-being that is often overlooked by companies. Employees who feel supported in their mental health are more engaged, motivated, and productive. Companies can offer mental health resources such as access to therapy sessions, mental health days, and wellness programs. By doing so, employees feel valued and supported, leading to better mental health outcomes and higher levels of employee satisfaction.

Paid time off is a crucial aspect of work-life balance that many employees value. I am not talking about 10 days of PTO. When companies offer a month or more of PTO, they allow employees to take a break from work and recharge, leading to higher levels of productivity and creativity when they return to work. PTO can reduce burnout and unnecessary stress,  which can impact employee well-being and job satisfaction.

Work-life balance is a critical component of employee satisfaction and retention. When employees feel they have control over their work schedules, they are more likely to feel motivated and productive. In contrast, employees who feel overworked and stressed are at risk of burnout, which can lead to low morale, absenteeism, and turnover. Companies can implement policies that encourage work-life balance, such as limiting after-hours emails and respecting employees' time off.

If you are in a position to, offer hybrid or remote to staff. We know that many employees have different things going on at home. For the employees that need flexibility, it will offer them it will offer many employees something that many a benefit many employers will not offer and it comes at little to no cost to the company.

Pay employees fair wages! Employees if they believe they are compensated well will not leave. Everyone needs to earn a fair income and to be happy with their income.

Companies must prioritize their employees' well-being to retain top talent. This includes offering mental health resources, generous paid time off, work-life balance policies, and hybrid or remote work arrangements. Prioritizing employee well-being leads to a happier, more productive, and loyal workforce, creating a positive workplace culture that attracts and retains top talent in today's competitive business environment.
