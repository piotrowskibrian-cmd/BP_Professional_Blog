Recruitment is a profession I truly love—but let’s be real, it’s not without its fair share of moments that make me cringe. Here are my 5 biggest “icks” in the hiring process—those things that make me want to throw my hands up in frustration. Whether you’re a recruiter, hiring manager, or candidate, I’m sure you’ve encountered some of these too.

1\. **The Overuse of “Rock Star”**

Can we *please* retire this term from the recruitment vocabulary? Candidates are professionals with a wealth of skills and experience, not lead singers in a hair band.

2\. **The MIA Hiring Manager**

Few things scream “we don’t value your time” louder than a hiring manager who shows up late to—or worse, doesn’t show up at all for—a scheduled interview. It’s not just unprofessional; it’s a missed opportunity to make a positive impression on the candidate. We’re in a competitive talent market. Respecting a candidate’s time is the bare minimum.

3\. **The Mystery of the Missing Salary Range**

Why are we still posting job ads without salary ranges? It’s 2025. Transparency around pay is no longer optional—it’s expected. Not only does withholding this information waste everyone’s time, but it also sends the wrong message about your company’s culture.

4\. **Cringe-Worthy Corporate Jargon**

“Work hard, play hard.” “We’re a family.” “Looking for a team player.” Every time I see phrases like these in a job description, I can’t help but cringe. Be specific about your culture and values - these phases make you look like every other company.

5\. **The Never-Ending Interview Process**

When the hiring process feels more like a marathon than a sprint, candidates (and recruiters) are bound to get frustrated. Requiring multiple rounds of interviews, endless panel discussions, and vague timelines is an instant recipe for candidate drop-off. If you can’t make a decision after three interviews, it’s time to reevaluate your process. Keep it streamlined, set clear expectations, and respect the candidate’s time. Long, drawn-out processes make your company look indecisive—and you risk losing top talent to faster-moving competitors.

6\. **The Broken Resume Parsing Tool**

In 2025 are we still talking about this? Nothing screams "we’re an innovative company" more than a broken or inaccurate resume parsing tool. It’s incredibly frustrating when a candidate's resume gets "read" incorrectly, with key skills and experience left out or misinterpreted by your ATS (Applicant Tracking System).

7\. **The Neglected Job Description**

There’s nothing worse than a job description that feels like a generic, cookie-cutter list of skills with no clear sense of what the role actually involves. Even worse? Misspellings and awkward phrasing that make your company look careless.

### What’s Your Biggest Ick?

Recruitment isn’t perfect, but we should aspire to be better. By calling out these little icks, we can work together to create a smoother, more respectful experience for everyone involved. After all, our ultimate goal is the same: connecting great talent with great opportunities.
