It may be a seemingly easy question to answer. However, like many questions, the answer is much complicated. Most recruiters function in a basic role of filling roles. Other recruiters will follow industry trends to see what companies are growing, laying off, and helping operations where possible to grow and better the organization. So what skills should a recruiter have? What makes a good recruiter, great?

One of the basic responsibilities of a recruiter is to fill requisition. Recruiters are responsible for working with managers to create job descriptions. Working with the hiring team to create a sourcing strategy to fill the role. When resumes start coming in the recruiters will review, phone interviews, and present candidates. Recruitment staff supports scheduling interviews and giving feedback from the hiring manager. After negotiating the offer, closing out the requisition, and giving feedback to candidates that won’t be receiving an offer.

Building a pipeline of candidates and networking is one of the basic skills of a recruiter. It really is the most effective way to fill requisitions if the recruiter has a network of candidates already. Without connecting with possible candidates; the recruiter won’t have a pulse on the market. If this task isn’t done recruiters won’t know what other companies are hiring, laying off, or the compensation of the market.

Communication is key to any relationship, right? Even in recruitment! Recruiters write countless emails, messages to candidates and descriptions. Remember recruiters are on the phone storytelling about their organizations and the positions they are looking to fill. To be able to fill roles you need to be able to communicate with a verity of people.

Recruiters should be coaching their client groups about candidates. Coaching can be anything from helping coach on what questions to ask, who they should interview and even who should get the offer. Sometimes recruiters need to coach and be the voice of reason. Being the individual to convince a manager to interview a candidate because they may not be a fit but they offer more.

In this industry, recruiters need to be very social media savvy. The recruitment industry keeps evolving with social media becoming more important. A recruiter needs to be able to post on social media, clear and engaging messages to be heard by followers. Knowing how to phrase content and what contact to share on what platforms.

The recruiters I have had the privilege of working it are some of the best researchers I have met. You’re tasked with finding candidates that may not want to be found through your typical means for searching. You must get creative and find ways to find candidates outside LinkedIn Boolean searches. Recruiters find names and contact information in many ways that include Google, LinkedIn, or Facebook.

It’s rare but the best recruiters having an in-depth knowledge of the market place. Recruiters will know what companies have pending lay-offs and how to leverage that to their advantage.

What other skills make up a recruiter? Did I miss any? Let me know in the comments.
