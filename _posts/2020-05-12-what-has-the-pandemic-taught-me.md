The last two weeks were an unintentional break from writing. With the COVID - 19 pandemic in the background it’s been hard to write about current recruitment trends or fads. Yes, things are changing on how we recruit but bigger issues of a global health pandemic have been on my mind. We all have fears due to the uncertainty of this virus. We all want to go back to our normal lives but we know it won’t be the same as before the pandemic. It’s been roughly two months since most of us have been told we are working from home for the next two weeks. Yes, that's right two weeks is now two months. In this time we have gotten accustomed to dogs barking and kids on zoom calls. At home, we’ve started binge-watching Netflix, video chatting with friends, and baking endless supplies of bread, cookies, cupcakes. To this point in this pandemic, what has this taught me?

1\. Resilient

Everyone has learned to adapt to this environment. We quickly had to learn to act differently around everyone in a very quick way. We’ve learned to wear a mask, stay 6ft away, and work from home. We naturally are very social individuals and want to be around people. We learn to adapt technology to communicate with friends. I’ve seen drive-in theaters open up again. People are using empty parking lots and backing cars in a circle and sitting on their tailgate to talk to friends.

2\. People Want to Work

With many businesses closed people have been wanting to go back to work. The original plan of being closed for two seems more of a vacation. Being two months in people want to work and earn an income. With many people on unemployment insurance, people would love to be productive members of society.

3\. Use Your Network

Many professional organizations are trying to create free or low-cost training to members. In talent acquisitions, I know of several companies and organizations providing training to skills sharp.

4\. People are willing to help

Just like professional organizations are trying to improve skills, friends, and neighbors are offering a helping hand. On my neighborhood Facebook page, people are giving away masks, offering to go run errands for “at-risk” population, lending libraries are turned into food pantries. People will help, you can’t be too proud to ask. We all need a helping hand sometimes.

5\. Technology is keeping us closer

I have never been more social than prior to this pandemic. I have been on more video calls with friends, coworkers, and family than ever before. We will only see this technology grow as Zoom, Teams, Hangouts all get better.

This list is only five items but I could sit here and write more. However, in my experience since this pandemic, this is what I have seen. If something is missing, feel free to put a comment below.
