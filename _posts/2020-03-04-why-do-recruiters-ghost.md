I should start by saying this isn’t a practice that I endorse or support. It is, however, important to explain why this happens but explore why excuses are just excuses.

For those that don’t know, ghosting is a practice where a relationship is ended without any communication from one of the individuals in the relationship. This practice has become more popular in recent years in dating. There is an argument that probably can be made recruiters have been practicing this before dating. However, with social media, we hear about it more often.

Let’s start with the easy one, people generally don’t like giving bad news. For example, the hiring manager tells the recruiter the candidate isn’t a fit. Now it’s the responsibility is to the recruiter to relay the information to the candidate. The recruiter is then tasked with delivering the bad news to the candidate. Very few people like delivering bad news but it is the responsibility of the recruiter to call with that news.

Too many recruiters would rather send the automatic rejection email to the candidate that says, “thanks but no thanks.” The excuse that you’ll hear from a recruiter is “compliance” and we may have a lawsuit if I give the feedback. To debunk that myth, there is a way to give feedback that does not put corporations or the recruiter at risk of a lawsuit. If recruiters are worried about a lawsuit, companies may want to rethink how they train their recruiters and hiring managers.

It’s been said that recruiters will say they don’t have the time to give feedback to the candidate. Recruiters’ responsibilities keep growing and they need to keep building their talent pipeline, scheduling interviews, meetings with hiring managers, etc. It’s easy to find one hour a week to call or write your emails to candidates. Part of recruiters’ responsibility is to give feedback to candidates and recruiters need to make it a priority.

It’s poor form not to provide feedback to a candidate if they interview at a company. There are always excuses not to provide feedback but there are more reasons why feedback should be given to the candidate. It is very poor for the reputation of the recruiter and the company. We as T.A. professionals need to do better by candidates.
