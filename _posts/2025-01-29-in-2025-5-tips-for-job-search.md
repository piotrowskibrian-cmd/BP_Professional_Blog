Job searching in 2025 is tough! Even though unemployment rates are low, hiring still is slow. We see it all over social media—finding a new position often feels like a full-time job in itself. If you’re feeling stuck and like you can’t get ahead, here are a few tips that might help.

1\. **Update Your Resume and Use AI**

You should be using technology to improve your work product—so why not your resume? Start by entering the job description into ChatGPT and let it analyze your resume, offering suggestions for improvement. It might seem daunting at first, but remember: recruiters often spend less than a minute reviewing a resume, so it’s crucial to make those few seconds count. Keep your resume well-formatted, easy to read, and focused on highlighting your key accomplishments.

Don’t stuff a bunch of keywords at the bottom of your resume to "beat" an ATS. Not only does this not work, but once someone reviews it, it’ll be obvious—and not in a good way.

Tip: Follow @Jonathanwordsofwisdom on Instagram if you're struggling with where to start on your resume.

2\. **Use Google for Jobs and Set Up Alerts**

Google for Jobs is an good tool because it pulls job listings from job boards, company websites, and other online sources. You can set up job alerts, and Google will email you at the cadence you select.

**Caution:** Be careful of scammy job listings or fake job boards -  there are always a few on Google.

3\. **How to Search LinkedIn**

We know that LinkedIn has a section for companies to post their openings but you can search LinkedIn another way for opportunities. For example, try searching with the following and then going to posts: ("Software Engineer" AND "New York" AND #hiring). This search will show you posts from hiring managers and recruiters who are actively looking for candidates. If you have a certain skillset you can replace the title with the skillset.

4\. **Apply and Reach Out to the Hiring Manager or Recruiter**

With so many job seekers out there, how do you stand out? Reach out to the recruiter or hiring manager on LinkedIn and send a personalized message that explains why you’re a great fit for the role. This extra effort shows that you’re proactive and truly excited about the opportunity. Not sure how to find the right person? Here’s a tip: search for the company in LinkedIn’s search bar, click on the company name, go to the company’s home page, select “People,” search for relevant titles there, and click connect.

5\. **Stay Organized with a Job Search Tracker**

Keep track of where you’ve applied and any next steps, use a job search tracker. You can easily create one in Excel or Numbers (for Mac users). Keep columns for the job title, company, location, key requirements, salary expectations, interview notes, etc. This will help you stay on top of your applications and ensure nothing falls through the cracks.

Remember, it's about working smarter, not harder!
