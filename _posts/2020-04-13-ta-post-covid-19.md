We all know, our world post COVID-19 will not be the same as it was prior to this pandemic. It will change our everyday life for the better, some will be worse, and some changes will just be changes we all will adapt to. For must of us, we are hoping we have hit our peak of cases and cases begin to reduce so we can enjoy the things we’ve missed. Letting us return to normal life. In the world of recruiting and talent acquisition what will change? How will this pandemic change the field we are in? Below are my 4 predictions for staffing and recruiting.

1\. Career Fairs Go Virtual

Conferences started to adapt to a virtual platform. Due to our current situation, the options were to postpone, cancel, or change the venue of the conferences. Many decided to change the venue to virtual events. Many career fairs will also follow this trend. At least for the foreseeable future, I see people being hesitant to be in large groups. Nothing seems worse than having a few hundred people in one large room shaking hands and being in close contact. The adoption of virtual career fairs has already happened. We will see it become a much more acceptable platform and universities will begin to adapt. This will allow candidates can speak with companies via a video chat from their house or dorm.

2\. Virtual Interviews added to the interview process

For the companies that are still interviewing virtual interviews have become normal. Video interviews have been around for a while now but due to COVID, we have been forced to adapt virtual interviews to the current process. Video interviews give you almost all the same abilities as face-to-face interviews without someone having to come into an office. This could allow developing earlier relationships with hiring managers and candidates. Video interviews could adopt asking a few quick questions that the company selects and the candidate records their answer and send back; to replacing phone interviews having candidates and hiring managers chat over cameras.

3\. Hiring Slows until a COVID Vaccine

Now, I am not an economist but our economies have basically stopped over the last few weeks. I don’t foresee our stay-at-home orders being lifted completely. Businesses and offices will open slowly, government officials will hesitate to reopen the entire economy until a better form of testing is available or a vaccine will come to market. This meaning not everyone will be going back to work right away. We will see a stimulus from the government to support growth but we will be slow to hire again. It will take time to regrow the economy and the growth we've lost these last few weeks.

4\. Workers Stay at Home

With many companies adopting working from home, you will see many implementing some type of telecommuting options. Whether this telecommute is one day a week or a fully remote position, we will see more people working from home. We’ve been doing it for the past month, everyone has adapted to it well. It’s a benefit that most employers can offer at very little cost to the employee. It also gives many employees more time in their day not commuting.

I assume most people aren’t surprised our world will go more virtual. We’ve been heading that way for years and current circumstances forced us to be more virtual. Our economies need time to recover. Until COVID-19 is well in our rearview mirror with a vaccine, I don’t foresee our economy running on all cylinders and hiring back many of the lost positions will take time.
