Recently, the United States government has made known its intent to great a ban on non-compete agreements. These agreements are often used to protect a company's trade secrets and confidential information, but they can also have a negative impact on workers and the economy as a whole.

One of the main reasons why non-compete agreements effect individuals negatively is they limit an employee's career opportunities. When an employee signs a non-compete agreement, they are effectively tied to their current employer and may not be able to take a new job in their field without risking legal action. This can make it difficult for workers to advance their careers, especially in industries where non-compete agreements are common, such as technology.

When employees are prevented from working for a competitor, they are not able to share their knowledge and skills with others in the industry. This can lead to a lack of innovation and a lack of competition, which can ultimately harm the economy.

Non-compete agreements can have a negative impact on small businesses. Small businesses often cannot afford to invest in the legal resources needed to enforce non-compete agreements, so they may be at a disadvantage when competing with larger companies that can. This can lead to a lack of competition and higher prices for consumers.

From a candidate's perspective, if they are offered two positions - one with no non-compete and the other requiring signing of a non-compete - it's unlikely they would choose the latter. Signing a document that restricts their career progression is not a desirable option for new hires.

I'm in favor having a federal ban on non-compete agreements. The negative impact on employees, the economy, small businesses and can lead to discrimination and bias. Therefore, non-compete agreements should be federally banned to protect the rights of workers and to promote innovation, competition, and fair business practices.
