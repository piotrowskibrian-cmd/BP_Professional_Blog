I've enjoyed writing this series on helping candidates in their job search and have a couple more article in this series. For now, let’s focus on how you can use ChatGPT to build your resume and land your next interview.

ChatGPT can do much more than just proofreading, grammar checks, and formatting—it can improve your resume in ways you might not have considered. Here’s how:

1\. Improve Your Bullet Points

Vague statements can be rewritten into well-structured, impactful bullet points that highlight your achievements. It can also help quantify your results to show how you contributed to your previous roles.

2\. Tailoring Your Resume for Your Next Position

You can ask ChatGPT to analyze your resume and suggest what improvements your resume needs for the title or position you are pursuing. For example, if you’re a Senior Software Engineer aiming for a Software Engineering Manager role, you might ask:

"Please review my resume and recommend how to improvement my resume to land a Software Engineering Manager role."

3\. Turning Performance Reviews into Resume Material

If you have past performance reviews or manager feedback, ChatGPT can transform them into strong, results-driven resume bullet points. This helps you showcase achievements backed by real feedback.

4\. Translating Soft Skills into Measurable Impact

Instead of stating "good communication skills," ChatGPT can rephrase it in a way that demonstrates real-world impact.  Such as, "Led cross-functional meetings, improving team alignment and reducing project turnaround time by 20%."

5\. Creating a Professional Summary

A strong summary at the top of your resume makes a recruiter and hiring manager read the rest of your resume. ChatGPT can help craft one that effectively communicates your experience, strengths, and career aspirations.

6\. Explaining Employment Gaps

If you took time off for caregiving, education, travel, or a sabbatical, ChatGPT can help you frame it in a way that doesn’t hurt your chances. Instead of focusing on the gap, it can highlight skills gained, personal growth, and relevant experiences during that time.

There are countless ways to leverage ChatGPT, and I’m a strong advocate for its use. Just don't forget to go back and ensure your resume is in your voice.
