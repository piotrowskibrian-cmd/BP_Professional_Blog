As we gather around the table this Thanksgiving, I’m taking a moment to reflect on the things I’m truly thankful for. Family? Of course. Good food? Absolutely. But as a recruiter, there are a few things I’m especially grateful for this year. Grab a PSL and let’s dive into this gratitude-filled post:

1\. The People Who Actually Show Up

Hiring managers, candidates, and new hires—all of us get caught up in meetings, and yes, sometimes we forget things. Your last call ran too long, your meeting reminder went off a little too late, or maybe your phone notifications are a disaster. But I am especially thankful for the candidates and hiring managers who show up on time, ready to go, and prepared. In the world of recruiting, that’s practically a superpower.

2\. Applicants Who Follow Directions

You’d be amazed how often something as simple as "Please attach your resume" can be misinterpreted as "Send me your social media profiles, links to your accounts, or even a 10-minute video about your life story." This year, I’m extra thankful for the candidates who read job descriptions, follow instructions to the letter, and send exactly what’s requested.

3\. Hiring Managers Who Give Timely and Constructive Feedback

It’s the age-old dilemma: I send over five amazing candidates, and then... *crickets*. But I’m thankful for the hiring managers who take the time to actually respond, provide detailed feedback, and don’t just say “Let me think about it” until 2027.

4\. Technology That Works

Let’s face it—recruiting sometimes feels like juggling flaming swords while blindfolded and walking a tightrope. But when my applicant tracking system is working flawlessly, my email doesn’t crash, and my interview scheduling link actually loads the first time? It’s like the universe is giving me a high five.

5\. A Special Thank You to My Coffee Maker

And finally, a very special shout-out goes to my coffee maker. Without you, I would not have the energy to screen resumes, crunch numbers in Excel, or stare at KPIs before 9 AM. Let’s be real—I’d probably still be asleep, dreaming about that perfect candidate (who also shows up on time with their resume updated).

So here’s to another year of finding the perfect match, making amazing connections, and sharing a virtual toast to the great work we do together.

**Happy Thanksgiving!**
