Talent Acquisition Day was started by the Association of Talent Acquisitions Professionals. It’s a day for overzealous professionals to celebrate their profession within recruitment. If you know me, I am one of those overzealous professionals. On this day (and every other day) thank your recruiter(s) that has helped you fill your requisitions.

When you have a hard-to-fill requisition and recruiters come in clutch to fill your requisition. Your recruiters know the market, your struggles, your selling points, and learn why people are leaving certain companies. Recruiters can help you understand your market for example if your internal compensation structure is out of line, who is hiring, who is laying off, etc...

For those in TA, go out and celebrate your profession. Learn something new from the many webinars today.
