Apple just released its new Apple Vision Pro. After the release, we have seen people wearing the goggles while driving, on trains, and walking through malls. Besides the immediate concern of less human interaction - I finally do believe the metaverse can actually happen.

In a virtual world, you can have people sit around a virtual table from all of the world. You can have architects create a building while walking through their virtual building and making redlines as they walk through with staff. You can have an engineer diagnose an issue with a machine on a factory floor by looking through a pair of VR goggles. An HVAC tech could look into a furnace before coming out to your home to fix an issue and to ensure they have the correct parts on hand.

Instead of screen sharing your metaverse can be shared with someone else to help walk you through your work problem.

A lot of technological improvement and adoption must happen before we get to this point. Cost is also a major issue, at a cost of $4,000 will be a barrier for many.
