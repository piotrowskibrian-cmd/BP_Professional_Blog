For the sake of this, let’s assume we aren’t speaking about recruiters that have business development responsibilities. We want to speak about recruiters that are responsible for souring and filling requisitions or job orders. Recruiters are responsible for finding possible candidates, talking to candidates about the job responsibility and their qualifications to make sure it's a fit for both parties. If a candidate is on the fence about a position or trying to convince a hiring manager to speak to a candidate can always be a challenge but what trait do you need? Sales, Storytelling or being a good listener?

Every company has a story to tell. Whether it’s about culture, projects, mission, values, or something else. Companies are providing some type of products or services to their clients. While non-profits help society somehow. Recruiters, in their respected organization, will tell the story about the company, projects they work on, products they sell, services they provide, or how they support the greater good of society. Recruiters also tell the candidate about the role they will be in and what the candidate's responsibilities are in the organization.

On the other side, the recruiter has to listen to candidates and the hiring manager's needs. If a recruiter is told to look for a front-end developer but ends up only showing DBA resumes, they probably didn’t listen to the hiring manger’s need. There may be an argument there that the recruiter isn't good at their job but we can save that for another day. Candidates will explain to recruiters why they are looking for a new position. If recruiters don’t listen to a candidate's needs and want then, the candidate may not take the offer because something is missing. The candidate could be missing development, promotion, a better work environment, compensation, a thank you, or a mission they can believe in. The list is endless of what a candidate wants in their career. It's the responsibility of the recruiter to listen and communicate that with the hiring manager.

I am not of fan of calling recruiters sales people but recruiters need to be. Let’s take for example a candidate that may not be a “perfect fit.” The candidate meets the minimum requirements but something is wrong. They may have job-hopped a little, the resume is formatted poorly, too light on software or standard that is a nice to have. Recruiter try to sway hiring managers to at least talk to a candidate. My favorite line to a hiring manager is, “Get on the phone with the candidate. If you don’t like the candidate then you can tell me I told you so. If you like them, invite the candidate in for a face-to-face and at least we didn’t miss out on a candidate.” Candidates are no different, we try to sell an opportunity through storytelling or by being salespeople.

Recruiters need to have all three to be successful recruiters. Did you expect a different answer? Probably not. These are not the only trait that makes up good recruiters but these are musts in a recruiters toolbox for success. Let me know your thoughts in the comments below.
