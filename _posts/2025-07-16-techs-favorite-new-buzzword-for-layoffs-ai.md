2024 and 2025 have been coated in tech layoffs and the list keeps growing.

Most recently, Indeed and Glassdoor cut another 1,300 employees. That marks their third major round of layoffs since 2023. Indeed isn't alone in layoff either. Microsoft has laid off over 15,000 in 2025. LinkedIn cut 281. Intel, ZoomInfo, Meta, and TikTok have all made recent reductions. Amazon’s CEO even stated they expect to shrink their workforce as AI efficiency grows.

According to [Layoffs.fyi](https://layoffs.fyi/), more than 95,000 tech jobs have been cut in 2025 alone and a growing number of those layoffs are being explicitly tied to AI, automation, and “tech efficiency.”

But Let’s Be Honest, AI isn’t always the true reason. It’s just the easiest one to say out loud and one one buzzword that

Many of these layoffs come down to old fashioned corporate priorities; cutting costs, consolidating teams, boosting shareholder value. AI just gives it a futuristic spin.

If 2023 was the year of AI hype, 2024 was the year of AI integration, then 2025 is the year companies began justifying job cuts by pointing at AI and in some cases, actually replacing people with it.

This isn't just a trend, AI is no longer just a tool. It's becoming model.
