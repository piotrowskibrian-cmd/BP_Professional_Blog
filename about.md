Brian Piotrowski is a Talent Acquisition executive with more than 15 years of experience building high-performing recruiting teams and modernizing the systems, technology, and processes behind great hiring. He currently leads talent acquisition for a 2,100-employee North American architecture, engineering, and construction firm, and previously led a 17-person global TA organization hiring across North America, the Pacific, Saudi Arabia, and Latin America.

His career spans corporate teams and search firms, with hiring across AEC, Oil & Gas, Manufacturing, Environmental Services, IT, and Technology. He starts by learning the business, then builds what makes TA more effective, measurable, and scalable: business-aligned recruiting teams, recruiter KPIs and dashboards, structured interviewing, early-career programs, and ATS and HCM implementations. He has also served on a corporate AI Governance Board, helping shape responsible automation and talent data strategy.

Through *The Offer Letter*, Brian shares real-world insights and candid opinions designed to spark conversation, challenge the "we've always done it that way" mindset, and support growth for recruiters, hiring leaders, and job seekers alike. On the podcast, *Candidate Spotlight*, he gives job seekers a voice and shares the stories behind their resumes.
